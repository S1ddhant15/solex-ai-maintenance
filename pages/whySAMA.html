SAMA – From Reactive Recovery to Autonomous Maintenance

SAMA (Solex AI Maintenance Assistant) is an intelligent maintenance platform designed to reduce machine downtime, preserve organizational knowledge, standardize troubleshooting, improve maintenance response and support the transition toward autonomous maintenance.

⚠ Current Challenges

Why a plant-level AI maintenance assistant is needed.

Expert Dependency

Troubleshooting often depends on a limited number of experienced engineers.

Slow Diagnosis

Time is lost identifying alarms, causes, manuals and previous solutions.

Knowledge Loss

Valuable maintenance experience remains with individuals instead of the organization.

Scattered Information

SOPs, manuals, breakdown records and spare information may exist in multiple locations.

Repeated Failures

Similar breakdowns can recur when previous root causes and corrective actions are not reused.

Production Impact

Higher MTTR and unplanned downtime directly affect machine availability, output and OEE.

🚀 SAMA Development Roadmap

A two-phase approach from digital assistance to intelligent autonomous maintenance.

PHASE 1

🤖 Digital AI Maintenance Assistant

Objective: Digitize maintenance knowledge and provide intelligent decision support to operators and maintenance teams.

  • AI-guided troubleshooting
  • Alarm diagnosis with probable causes
  • Recommended corrective actions
  • ATW Stringer maintenance knowledge
  • SC Laminator maintenance knowledge
  • Preventive maintenance checklists
  • Spare part recommendations
  • Breakdown history reference
  • Repeated failure analysis
  • Machine health information
  • English / Hindi / Gujarati support
Business Impact

Faster diagnosis • Reduced MTTR • Standardized troubleshooting • Knowledge retention • Reduced dependency on individuals

PHASE 2

🧠 Intelligent & Autonomous Maintenance

Objective: Connect SAMA with real equipment and plant data to move from reactive support toward predictive and autonomous maintenance.

  • PLC & machine integration
  • Live alarm monitoring
  • Real-time machine health
  • Temperature and vacuum monitoring
  • Servo / sensor condition monitoring
  • Automatic fault detection
  • AI-assisted root cause analysis
  • Predictive maintenance
  • Automatic maintenance reports
  • Spare inventory integration
  • MES / MySQL / ERP integration
  • Continuous learning from resolved failures
Business Impact

Reduced unplanned downtime • Higher availability • Improved OEE • Better spare planning • Data-driven maintenance • Autonomous decision support

Operational improvements enabled through a centralized AI maintenance ecosystem.
