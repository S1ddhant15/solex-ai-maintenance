🤖 SAMA – From Reactive Recovery to Autonomous Maintenance

SAMA (Solex AI Maintenance Assistant) is an AI-powered maintenance platform designed to reduce machine downtime, preserve maintenance knowledge, standardize troubleshooting and improve maintenance response across the manufacturing plant.

⚠️ Current Challenges

Expert Dependency

Troubleshooting depends heavily on experienced maintenance engineers.

Slow Diagnosis

Fault identification can increase machine downtime and MTTR.

Knowledge Loss

Maintenance experience often remains with individuals instead of the organization.

Scattered Information

SOPs, manuals and maintenance records are stored across multiple locations.

Repeated Failures

Previous breakdown knowledge may not be readily available during recurring problems.

Production Impact

Longer downtime directly impacts machine availability, production and OEE.

🚀 SAMA Development Roadmap

PHASE 1

🤖 Digital AI Maintenance Assistant

Objective: Digitize maintenance knowledge and provide intelligent troubleshooting support.

  • AI-guided machine troubleshooting
  • Alarm diagnosis with probable causes
  • Recommended corrective actions
  • Preventive maintenance checklists
  • Intelligent spare recommendations
  • Machine health information
  • Breakdown history reference
  • Centralized maintenance knowledge
  • Multi-language support
Business Impact

Faster diagnosis • Reduced MTTR • Standardized troubleshooting • Reduced dependency on individuals

PHASE 2

🧠 Intelligent & Autonomous Maintenance

Objective: Connect SAMA with live equipment data and move toward predictive and autonomous maintenance.

  • PLC & machine integration
  • Live machine health monitoring
  • Vacuum pressure monitoring
  • Temperature monitoring
  • Real-time alarm monitoring
  • AI-assisted root cause analysis
  • Predictive maintenance
  • Automatic maintenance reports
  • MES / ERP integration
  • Continuous AI learning
Business Impact

Reduced unplanned downtime • Higher machine availability • Improved OEE • Data-driven maintenance

Transform maintenance at Solex from Reactive Recovery to Intelligent & Autonomous Maintenance — improving reliability, reducing downtime, preserving organizational knowledge and driving operational excellence.
